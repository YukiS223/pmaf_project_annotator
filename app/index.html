How to annotate

  1. Create a track per PMAF (N). The track keeps its ID for the whole life of that PMAF across frames.
  2. Circle the form (E): hold the mouse button and trace loosely around it; release to close the outline. Drag inside the outline to move it; drawing again (starting outside it) replaces it.
  3. S snaps a pixel mask to the bright aurora inside your outline (the mask never grows beyond it). Adjust the threshold slider — the preview updates live; fix details with the brush (B add, X erase).
  4. A accepts the visible mask and advances — the tool copies the outline forward and shows a preview snap, which you adjust and accept. Previews are never saved: only what you explicitly Accept (or mark Absent) is stored. Never accept a preview you have not looked at.
  5. If the PMAF is not visible on a frame, press V (absent). A frame exports only when every track is accepted or absent on it. Use Starts here / Ends here to mark everything before / after the PMAF's life absent in one click (both undoable).
  6. Save is automatic — always, even without exporting. Relaunching the app reopens your project exactly where you left off.
